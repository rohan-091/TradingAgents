# Workflow: STORM Multi-Perspective Research

Execute deep research on `[TOPIC]` using the **Stanford OVAL STORM Method**:

1. **Phase 1 — Multi-Perspective Scan**:
   - Practitioner (execution reality, operational friction)
   - Academic (peer-reviewed empirical evidence)
   - Skeptic (contrarian risks and falsification tests)
   - Economist (incentives, profit motives, capital flows)
   - Historian (precedents and cyclical patterns)

2. **Phase 2 — Contradiction Map**:
   - Clashing claims, evidence weighting, crux question, universal consensus, and systemic blind spots.

3. **Phase 3 — Synthesis Briefing**:
   - 60-second executive summary, 5 prioritized findings, hidden non-obvious connection, role-specific action directive, frontier horizon question.

4. **Phase 4 — Peer Review & Self-Critique**:
   - Confidence scoring (1-10), weakest link falsification test, bias audit, missing 6th perspective, letter grade and revision vector.
