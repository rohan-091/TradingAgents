## 2. Company Overview & Business Architecture

### 2.1 Business Model & Products

Incorporated in July 2001, KP Green Engineering Limited manufactures fabricated and hot-dip galvanized steel products. [1]


#### Key Operational Highlights

- Part of KP Group[1]The company is a part of the KP Group, promoted by Dr. Faruk G. Patel. The KP Group operates four 4 entities spanning the renewable energy and infrastructure value chain, including KP Green Engineering (steel structure manufacturing), KPI Green Energy (solar/hybrid IPP and captive power), KP Energy (wind BoP solutions), and KPI Green Hydrogen and Ammonia (green hydrogen). The group has an installed renewable energy capacity of over2.5+ GWand has set a long-term target of scaling this to10+ GWby 2030.
